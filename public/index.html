📖 Audiobook Prep Stage 1

Deterministic cleaning & chunking of public-domain books for ElevenLabs. Batch-load several at once, OCR scanned PDFs in-browser, and optionally push the results straight to a repo branch.

How to use this — click to expand (read me if it's been a while)

This is Stage 1 of prepping public-domain books for ElevenLabs. It cleans the text, splits it into narration-sized chunks, lists likely mispronunciations, and adds a few structural pauses — then either hands you a ZIP or commits the results to the book-chunks branch of the repo.

  1. Add books. Drag in (or click to pick) one or many .txt, .epub, or .pdf files. They queue up below.
  2. Leave both checkboxes on unless you have a reason not to: Auto-OCR (reads scanned PDFs) and structural breaks.
  3. To push to the repo (optional): type the app password and click Connect. Skip this to just download a ZIP instead. (The password is the APP_PASSWORD secret set on the Cloudflare Worker.)
  4. Click “Start processing” and you can walk away. It works through the queue one book at a time, OCR'ing scans as needed, and — if connected — commits each finished book to the branch as it completes. Keep this tab in the foreground (background tabs get suspended by the browser).
  5. When it's done: if you didn't connect, click Download all as ZIP. If you did, the books are already on the book-chunks branch under chunks/<book>/.

What each book produces (in its chunks/<book>/ folder):

  • ch001_part01.txt … — the chunked text to narrate, in order.
  • _PRONUNCIATION.tsv — names/terms a voice may mispronounce, most-frequent first. Open it, fill the alias column with a plain phonetic respelling (e.g. Qabalah → Kah-BAH-lah), and load those as alias rules in ElevenLabs. Plain respellings, not IPA — IPA/phoneme rules only work on the V2-flash model and are ignored elsewhere.
  • _REVIEW_FLAGS.txt — ambiguous bits the script wouldn't guess on (St. = Saint/Street?, times, ordinals…), for the Stage 2 review.

Then what? Stage 2 is a Claude Code pass: ask it to “review the book-chunks branch” — it resolves the review flags, adds expressive pauses, and writes the finished final/<book>/ chunks you actually narrate in ElevenLabs.

Privacy: all text processing and OCR happen in your browser. Nothing is uploaded unless you connect and push — and then only the finished chunk text goes to your own Worker. No data is stored; reloading the page clears everything.
