The teaching and explanatory method, generally known as re-education, is chiefly associated with the name of Du Bois, who was its original exponent, but as, in his day, the psychoanalytic method of investigating the causes of mental disease was unknown, he was often groping in the dark, and dealing with secondary symptoms and effects, so that his method fell into disrepute in the eyes of the new school. That this method, wisely handled, can be of great benefit in expediting a cure and lessening the painfulness of the process, is beyond gainsay.
